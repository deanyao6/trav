## Top 5 research insights

1. Horizontal agents are now in group chats: Instinct launched a group agent on 2026-10-05 whose pitch is "snag tickets, split the cost". Trav has to win on depth: the ledger, approvals, multi-origin flights and receipts.
2. ChatGPT group chats were retired (wind-down from 2026-07-09). Reviews complained the bot interrupted constantly with paragraph-long replies. Trav's terse, mention-only voice is the answer.
3. Travel AI incumbents are solo and pre-trip. Mindtrip comes closest (an `@mindtrip` bot and in-chat flight checkout), but it lives in its own app and does nothing during or after the trip.
4. Expense splitters are mature but manual, and Splitwise's paywalls (an entry cap, OCR and FX moved to Pro) are pushing users away. None lives in the chat or captures expenses automatically.
5. The trust gap: 89% want AI help planning, but only 12% are comfortable letting AI decide. Trav's approval policy and audit log are selling points.

## Connector feasibility

- **iMessage groups:** reachable only through unofficial relays on Apple hardware (Photon, Linq, Sendblue); there is no official API. This is the biggest platform risk. The hedge is WhatsApp Groups (official, max 8 members, no buttons) or Telegram.
- **Tickets:** there's no public purchase API for Razzmatazz, Xceed, DICE or the Sagrada Família, so the real "buy and send QR codes" needs a business deal.
- **Flights:** Duffel is the only self-serve flight booking API with a real sandbox (Amadeus Self-Service closed 2026-07-17).
- **Lodging:** Airbnb is closed, so only wholesalers (Hotelbeds, Expedia Rapid, Duffel Stays) are bookable.
- **Settle-up:** must be request + deep link + mark paid. Moving money between friends would make Trav a money transmitter.
- **Card and email data:** Plaid works but needs compliance work. Reading Gmail needs a restricted-scope review plus an annual CASA assessment; a per-member forwarding address avoids it.
- **Going live:** anything Trav actually books needs an entity and a merchant-of-record arrangement.
