# T-069 + T-071 — Channel profiles and outbound pacing for relay lines (done)

D-048. Builder rounds: 2. The Orchestrator fixed 2 of the r2 minors.

T-069:
- `ChannelCapabilities` gains `seesAllGroupMessages`, `typingIndicator`, `readReceipts`, `groupTransport` and `canRenameGroup`.
- Three profiles: `simulator` (the default, unchanged), `imessage_relay_group` and `mixed_group_mms`.
- `textForChannel` rewrites answer instructions only on messages with actions or answer cards (`👍 to book` → `reply "yes" to book`; the digest line → "reply with the numbers you want on the tab"). Quoted text is never rewritten.
- End to end, the flagship works on both relay profiles.

T-071:
- `withOutboundPacing` caps a line at 500 sends a day, with sends ≤ max(10, 3× receives).
- Content-free acks go out as tapbacks.
- When full, messages queue by priority: critical (approvals, urgent), then normal, then low.
- A queued message refused by the relay is retried up to 3 times, then audited and reported to `onFailure`.
- Critical messages get a reserve of 20 slots, scaled down on small caps.
- Queued cards expire at the earlier of `expiresAt` and the proposal hold.
- Ids are bounded at 5,000 and the queue at 1,000 (stale normal entries are dropped with an audit; critical ones never are).
- `withInboundDebounce` merges one sender's messages within 3 seconds (held 10 seconds at most). Attachments never merge, and merged events carry `mergedMessageIds`.
- Both decorators use only the injected clock. Neither is wired into the simulator runtime by default.
