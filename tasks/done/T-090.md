# T-090 + T-104: Consent gate on document intake; only booking parties approve

**Verdict:** r1 CHANGES_REQUESTED (T-090 sound; 3 majors in the T-104 approver fallback: a minor's own request had no approvers, the fallback ignored `policy.approvers`, and the requester couldn't approve a charge on their own card; 2 minors) → r2 builder fixed all → accepted at the round cap. `pnpm check` green (3249), demo 40 of 40.

## What shipped
- **T-090:** `agent.intakeDocument` checks the owner's cardImport/emailImport consent before reading anything. If consent is off: no pending item, no card, no ledger write, one owner-only `private.item_dismissed{consent_missing|minor|not_eligible}` audit row, and a one-line DM for eligible adults. Receipt photos a member posts themselves are not gated. The simulator puts an un-consented document back on the rail.
- **T-104:** `bookingParties` = attendees + card owner(s) + guardians of minor attendees. `any_one` approvers are the pool (named approvers) ∩ parties, plus the adult requester and guardians; the fallback never leaves the list empty while an adult exists. The handler refuses non-party taps with `approval.invalid_attempt{not_attendee}`. Henry can approve his own-card request but not one on Dean's card. Multi-approver line: "any one of … can ok it". SAFETY §2.1 updated; D-028.
