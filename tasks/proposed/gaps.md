# Proposed tasks: gap-hunter

_Source: research/gaps.md (2026-10-22). The Orchestrator assigns T-numbers and priority order. Every item is a Trav product feature. None of these duplicate tasks/proposed/market.md; where a gap strengthens an M-task, see "Notes on existing tasks" at the bottom._

## Proposed

- [ ] G-01 | P0 | Activity roster: in-chat roll call drives both headcount and split | source: gaps | acceptance: "@trav who's in tonight?" (or any activity proposal) posts a one-tap roll call. Replies (in/out/maybe) and natural language ("Henry's sick") update the `Activity` roster. Any booking or expense tied to that activity uses the roster for BOTH ticket quantity and split participants, and a test proves they can't diverge (e.g. 5 tickets ⇒ split among exactly those 5). "Maybe" counts as out for purchases until confirmed. Tests: Razzmatazz 5-of-6, a mid-flow roster change before approval re-prices the proposal, and an empty roster blocks the purchase.
- [ ] G-02 | P0 | Pre-purchase consistency check and five-field approval card | source: gaps | acceptance: before any approval card, a pure function checks the proposed booking against trip context: city matches the trip city, dates are within the trip, quantity equals the activity roster, every named attendee is a group member, and no under-18 member is included in nightlife or 18+ items. Any failure → Trav asks one clarifying question and shows no buy option. The approval card always shows what/where, when, who (names + count), all-in total AND all-in per person in the trip currency (fees and FX included), and cancellation terms. Tests: a wrong-city item (Naples FL vs. Italy-style mismatch), date outside the trip, quantity ≠ roster, minor + club, and a passing Razzmatazz case.
- [ ] G-03 | P0 | Group-chat message budget enforced in code | source: gaps | acceptance: a channel-layer guard limits Trav to (a) replies to @mentions and direct follow-ups, (b) at most N proactive messages per group per simulated day (default 2, configurable in settings; urgent booking-change alerts exempt but logged), and (c) a max line count per group message (default 6), with longer content rendered as a collapsible card. Over-budget proactive messages are dropped or merged, never sent. Unit tests for each limit, plus an eval grader that fails any transcript exceeding the budget.
- [ ] G-04 | P1 | Trip close-out: loose-ends sweep, late-expense window, delta settle | source: gaps | acceptance: on the last trip day Trav posts one close-out message listing unsplit bookings and a COUNT of each member's pending private items (never their contents). Books close after a configurable window (default 48h). An expense added after the close yields only the incremental transfers vs. the closed settlement, not a new full list. `@trav show the math` lists each member's paid, owed, and net, summing to zero. Tests: the close-out message has no private item details; a delta after a late €40 taxi changes only the affected transfers; the math view reconciles to the cent.
- [ ] G-05 | P1 | Drop-out policy stated before booking, re-split on roster change | source: gaps | acceptance: approval cards for shared bookings include the drop-out rule (refundable share → mock-cancel and re-split among the rest; non-refundable → the drop-out still owes unless the group overrides). When a member drops after purchase, Trav applies the stated rule, posts a one-line ledger change, and audit-logs it. The override needs explicit confirmation from the member who would absorb the cost (the payer). Tests: refundable drop (re-split, mock refund recorded), non-refundable drop (share retained), and the override path.
- [ ] G-06 | P1 | Commit-before-book for large shared bookings | source: gaps | acceptance: for shared bookings above a configurable threshold (e.g. lodging), Trav posts a commit card ("Reply ✅ to lock your spot: €X each, refundable until D"). The booking stays pending until the required committers reply. On commit, each member's share is recorded as owed in the ledger. If commitments don't arrive by the deadline, the booking is not made and Trav reports who is outstanding. Purchases still go through ApprovalPolicy. No pre-charging (mock only). Tests: full commit → booking; a partial commit at the deadline → no booking; a commit withdrawn before the purchase.
- [ ] G-07 | P1 | Trav-sent settle-up nudges (no awkward asks) | source: gaps | acceptance: the daily settle-up phrases open transfers neutrally from Trav's voice ("3 transfers left to close Barcelona"). Each debtor gets at most 1 private nudge per day and at most 3 per trip, and a nudge stops as soon as the transfer is marked paid. Transfers below a configurable floor are netted or deferred, never nudged alone. Messages follow VOICE.md and never shame anyone in the group thread (outside opt-in roast mode guardrails). Tests: the nudge cap, stop-on-paid, and the small-amount floor.
- [ ] G-08 | P1 | Payment-rail-aware settle-up (mock deep links) | source: gaps | acceptance: each member privately sets accepted payout methods (e.g. Venmo, Revolut, Wise, PayPal, Bizum, cash), stored in their personal store and not shown to the group beyond what's needed. Settle-up lines show a rail both parties support ("Leo → Chase €31 via Revolut"), with a mock deep link. When no common rail exists Trav says so and suggests cash or a common app. No real payment calls. Tests: a US-only Venmo member paying an EU member picks a shared rail or flags none; privacy test that unused methods aren't revealed.
- [ ] G-09 | P1 | Group polls with deadline and default | source: gaps | acceptance: "@trav help us pick X" posts at most 3 options as a one-tap poll with a stated deadline. At the deadline Trav announces the winner (ties broken by a stated rule) and offers to book it through the normal approval flow. It never auto-purchases on deadline. Only current group members' votes count, one per member, and changes are allowed before close. Tests: tie-break, a late vote ignored, and no purchase without approval.
- [ ] G-10 | P1 | Revoke means purge, plus trip-end data minimization | source: gaps | acceptance: revoking a source ("@trav stop reading my card/email") deletes that member's raw unconfirmed items from storage (confirmed shared ledger items stay). At trip close, raw imported card and email payloads for all members are deleted, keeping only confirmed ledger entries. A member's DM "@trav what do you have on me" lists exactly the stored categories and counts. Tests verify deletion at the storage layer (both in-memory and SQLite impls) and that the group ledger is unaffected.
- [ ] G-11 | P1 | Cross-trip group memory, visible and editable | source: gaps | acceptance: at trip close Trav saves group-level facts (destinations, activity types attended, typical daily spend per person, stated preferences, group inside jokes only if surfaced in group chat) to group memory, never personal-store data. On a new trip with the same group, Trav's kickoff message references at most 3 remembered facts. `@trav what do you remember about us` lists them, and `@trav forget <item>` removes one with confirmation. Tests: personal data is never written to group memory; forget removes the item from future prompts.
- [ ] G-12 | P1 | Day-of logistics resurfacing | source: gaps | acceptance: driven by the simulator clock, each attendee of a ticketed activity privately receives their own QR code within a configurable window before start (default 60 min), and the group gets lodging check-in details on arrival day. Messages count against the G-03 proactive budget (per-person ticket DMs don't count against the group budget). Non-attendees get nothing. Tests: Henry (out) receives no Razzmatazz QR, all 5 attendees do, and timing respects the window.

## Notes on existing tasks (strengthen, don't duplicate)
- **M-11 / T-014 (flight optimizer):** add the *return-leg* spread when members fly home to different cities (MOTIVATION.md's exact case), plus a per-person fairness constraint (no member pays more than a configurable % above the group median unless they opt in). Competitors (Mindtrip Flights, Holiwise, PanFlights) align arrivals only.
- **M-03 (receipt from audit log):** also answer "@trav what did you just do?" from the audit log only. Instinct's early users complain about inaccurate self-explanations.
- **M-08 / T-011 (receipt photo):** Apple's iOS 27 Wallet bill split ships this for US Apple Cash users. Keep it, but demo it as part of the *trip* ledger (attendance + multi-expense + non-US members), not as a standalone trick.
- **Market.md correction:** ChatGPT group chats were retired starting 2026-07-09 (see research/gaps.md). Update the competitor table before it's used in the pitch.
