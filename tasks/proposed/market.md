# Proposed tasks: market-researcher

_Source: research/market.md (2026-10-22). The Orchestrator assigns T-numbers and priority order. Every item is a Trav product feature._

## Proposed

- [ ] M-01 | P0 | Approval policy: three decision tiers with a cited rule | source: market | acceptance: `ApprovalPolicy.evaluate()` returns `auto_approve` / `needs_confirm` / `blocked` plus the id of the rule that decided it. Auto-approve only when refundable AND under the per-person and per-group caps. Above the cap → needs_confirm. Non-refundable above the hard cap → blocked unless there's an explicit override confirmation. Every result is written to the audit log with the rule id. Unit tests cover all three tiers and the override path, and assert that no mock purchase runs without an approve or confirm record.
- [ ] M-02 | P0 | Booking options filtered and ranked by policy | source: market | acceptance: mock ticket/lodging/flight search results are filtered by the group's approval policy before Trav presents them. Refundable options rank first at equal fit. Each option shown carries a one-line reason (e.g. "refundable, €22/person, under cap"). Tests prove that options over the hard cap are never shown as one-tap buyable.
- [ ] M-03 | P0 | Post-action receipt generated from the audit log | source: market | acceptance: after any booking, the receipt message in chat is rendered from the audit-log entry (what was bought, total, per-person amount, who was charged, which rule approved it, refundable yes/no), not written freely by the model. A test confirms the receipt matches the ledger and the audit entry for the Razzmatazz scenario (5 of 6 members, Henry excluded).
- [ ] M-04 | P0 | Ledger: full split modes, including multiple payers | source: market | acceptance: supports equal, exclude-members, weighted shares, exact amounts, itemized, and expenses with more than one payer. Rounding remainders are assigned deterministically, so amounts always sum to the total. At least 2 tests per mode, plus a property test showing balances sum to zero.
- [ ] M-05 | P0 | Multi-currency trip ledger | source: market | acceptance: each expense stores its original amount and currency plus the FX rate used (a mock rate table). Balances and settle-up show in the group's trip currency, and Trav names the rate in chat on request. Tests cover a EUR trip with a USD card charge.
- [ ] M-06 | P0 | Minimal-transfer settle-up with suggested repayments | source: market | acceptance: settlement produces at most n-1 transfers and never more than a naive pairwise settlement. The daily settle-up message lists "X → Y: amount" lines in the trip currency. Tests include the 6-person Barcelona fixture and an already-settled group (zero transfers).
- [ ] M-07 | P0 | Private "looks shared" confirmation for card and email items | source: market | acceptance: imported card transactions and parsed Uber/Lyft emails are visible only to their owner as pending suggestions. The owner gets a one-tap "share with group / keep private" prompt, and only a confirmed item enters the group ledger or chat. Tests prove another member's view, and Trav's group replies, never include unconfirmed items. The owner can turn off the import source at any time, and existing pending items are then dropped.
- [ ] M-08 | P0 | Receipt photo → itemized "who had what?" flow | source: market | acceptance: a receipt image (mock vision output in offline mode) becomes line items plus tax and tip. Trav posts a compact itemized message asking members to claim items, unclaimed items default to an equal split among those present, and tax and tip are split pro rata. Tests cover a 3-item, 4-person dinner with one person excluded.
- [ ] M-09 | P0 | Per-group settings readable and editable in chat | source: market | acceptance: `@trav settings` shows roast mode (off by default), spending caps, trip currency, and reply mode (mention-only by default). Changing any money or roast setting requires confirmation from the member who made the request, and the change is written to the audit log. Turning on roast mode is refused if any member is under 18. Tests cover each setting and the minor block.
- [ ] M-10 | P0 | Group-only memory with per-member opt-in for personal data | source: market | acceptance: the group memory store and per-member personal stores are separate modules. Card, email, and photo access each need an explicit per-member opt-in recorded with a timestamp, and can be revoked in chat ("@trav stop reading my card"). Tests prove that group memory queries can't return personal-store data that hasn't been shared.
- [ ] M-11 | P0 | Flight optimizer returns a short list with reasons | source: market | acceptance: the multi-origin optimizer returns at most 3 group itineraries, each with a one-line rationale (arrival spread, departure spread, total cost, worst layover) and a refundable flag. Tests on the 6-origin Barcelona fixture check that the top pick beats the naive "cheapest per person" plan on arrival spread.
- [ ] M-12 | P1 | Read-only ledger web view | source: market | acceptance: the simulator serves a read-only ledger page per group at a local URL (127.0.0.1), showing balances, expenses, and suggested transfers. It shows only group-confirmed items, never pending personal ones. Trav posts the link on "@trav balances". Test: unconfirmed items are absent from the page data.
- [ ] M-13 | P1 | People-centric Trip Wrapped superlatives | source: market | acceptance: Wrapped includes the trip total, the biggest-spend day, the top category, "most fronted" (the member who paid the most up front), the most-visited place, and night-out count, plus up to N photos from opted-in members only. All superlatives are computed from ledger and trip data, never invented, and roast lines appear only if roast mode is on and pass the guardrails. Snapshot test on the Barcelona fixture.
- [ ] M-14 | P1 | Membership-change guard | source: market | acceptance: when a member joins mid-trip, Trav doesn't share earlier balances or past private confirmations until the newcomer is added to the ledger and an existing member acknowledges in chat. When a member leaves, they're excluded from future splits but their balance is kept. Tests cover join and leave.
- [ ] M-15 | P1 | Refundable-booking watch and disruption nudge (mock) | source: market | acceptance: for refundable mock lodging or flights Trav booked, a scheduled check against the mock provider detects a price drop or schedule change and posts one proactive message offering a rebook or an alternative. The rebook still goes through ApprovalPolicy. Proactive messages are limited to 1 per booking per day. Tests use mock provider events.
