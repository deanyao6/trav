# Proposed tasks from connector-scout (2026-10-22)

Interfaces referenced below are in `packages/adapters/src/interfaces/`. Feasibility notes are in `research/connectors.md`.

- [ ] C-01 | P0 | Mock ticket provider implementing `TicketsAdapter` with Barcelona inventory (Razzmatazz club nights, Barca match, Sagrada Familia, Park Guell): hold -> purchase -> one IssuedTicket per holder with a fake QR payload; `getTicketForHolder` returns only that member's ticket | source: connectors | acceptance: deterministic fixture inventory; sold-out, hold-expired, price-changed and redirect_only cases tested; purchase rejects missing/expired/insufficient ApprovalProof; same idempotencyKey never creates a second order
- [ ] C-02 | P0 | Per-person ticket delivery: after a purchase, Trav posts the receipt card in the group and sends each attendee only their own QR via `ChannelAdapter.sendDirect`; if the channel can't DM, it posts a per-person claim prompt that never reveals other members' codes | source: connectors | acceptance: Razzmatazz test with 5 holders yields 5 DMs, each containing exactly one barcode; no barcode payload ever appears in a group message
- [ ] C-03 | P0 | Channel capability fallback: every RichCard renders to `fallbackText` with reply keywords, and text replies ("YES", "NO", "1", "2") resolve to the same card actions as button taps on button-less channels (WhatsApp groups, SMS) | source: connectors | acceptance: simulator can run in "no buttons" mode and the Razzmatazz approval completes via text reply; unit tests for keyword-to-action mapping including ambiguous replies
- [ ] C-04 | P0 | Mock flight provider implementing `FlightsAdapter` with Duffel-shaped offers for six Barcelona origins (expiring offers, refundable vs non-refundable conditions, re-price before order) feeding the multi-origin optimizer | source: connectors | acceptance: offers expire on the simulator clock; createOrder fails with offer_expired/price_changed when appropriate; optimizer integration test consumes mock search results for 6 origins
- [ ] C-05 | P0 | Plaid-shaped mock transactions implementing `TransactionsAdapter.sync` with cursor paging, including pending -> posted transitions (e.g. a ride charge whose amount changes after tip) and refunds as credits | source: connectors | acceptance: fixtures cover added/modified/removed; a posted transaction replacing a pending one updates the private pending item instead of duplicating it; a member's sync never returns another member's transactions
- [ ] C-06 | P0 | Settle-up via payment requests (`SettleUpAdapter`): daily settle-up turns minimal transfers into requests with payment-method deep links built from each payee's handles; status flows pending -> marked_paid -> confirmed, and only confirmed requests settle the ledger | source: connectors | acceptance: Trav never claims money moved; ledger balances change only on payee confirmation; recomputing transfers cancels stale requests; tests for disputed and cancelled requests
- [ ] C-07 | P1 | Per-member receipt forwarding address: Trav DMs each member their private forwarding address; mock `EmailReceiptSource` routes fixture emails to the owner; parsed receipts become owner-private pending items with a one-tap "share with group" prompt | source: connectors | acceptance: address shown only in DM; emails with instruction-like text are parsed as data and flagged in `warnings`; address rotation stops old-address delivery
- [ ] C-08 | P1 | Mock lodging provider implementing `LodgingAdapter` with Barcelona whole-apartment and hotel rates (recheck-required rates, refundable-until deadlines, pay-at-property city tax) and a "message the host" path that returns not_supported for Airbnb-style listings so Trav offers a deep link and a check-in summary instead | source: connectors | acceptance: refundable-first ranking; recheck price change surfaces a re-approval; not_supported handled gracefully in chat
- [ ] C-09 | P1 | Multi-currency ledger support via `FxRatesAdapter` (fixed fixture rates in v0): expenses keep their original currency, and balances and settle-up convert to the group's home currency with the rate date shown | source: connectors | acceptance: EUR dinner + USD flight trip settles correctly to the cent; rate date shown on the settle-up card; weekend expense uses the previous business day's rate
- [ ] C-10 | P2 | Consent-gated album ingestion from chat: photos posted in the group by members who opted in are added to the trip album (location stripped unless allowed); non-consenting members' photos are ignored and Trav offers a private opt-in DM once | source: connectors | acceptance: tests prove photos from non-consenting members are never stored or returned; revoking consent hides photos from recaps immediately
